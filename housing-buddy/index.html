What I track

Half 1 · Valuation

📈 What your home is worth

A grounded view of a home's value and how it's changed.

  • Aggregated estimates, shown as ranges — never a fake-precise price
  • Historical trends and comparable sales
  • Saved-home pulse: what changed, and notable movement alerts
Half 2 · Search

🔎 Where you should live next

Requirements-based home search with a 360° area view.

  • Hard constraints + weighted preferences, tradeoffs in plain language
  • Ranked matches: new, updated, and removed options
  • Area view: schools, commute, safety, walkability, hazards

Key decisions

D1 · LEAD

Valuation first. The saved-home pulse is the daily habit; AVM data is the most reliable input. Search ships as the second tab.

D2 · AUDIENCE

Homeowners first, buyers second. Renters in v2 — the rental data path is already warm.

D3 · GEOGRAPHY

Sacramento metro for v1. Home market; its urban-to-exurban mix stress-tests sparse-data handling early. Bay Area next.

D4 · MATCHING

Hard constraints + weighted preferences. Tradeoffs explained in plain language, never a black-box score.

Data stack

Lawful, self-serve, no licensing negotiation — Zillow/Redfin/Realtor.com offer no public APIs, so v1 runs on:

RentCast API · core · $74–199/mo Zillow Research CSVs · free Walk Score · free tier Census ACS · free NCES schools · free FBI Crime Data · free FEMA hazards · free

Estimated v1 data cost: ~$75–200/mo. Parallel track: Zillow Bridge application + licensed-agent IDX partnership for MLS-complete inventory in v2.
