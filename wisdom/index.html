The revival thesis

Why it stalled

The system worked. What died was momentum: no feedback from Quentin and Sawyer, and Dave's own interest faded. So this round isn't about finishing the automation backlog — it's an experiment in engagement. The central question is the feedback loop, not the plumbing.
